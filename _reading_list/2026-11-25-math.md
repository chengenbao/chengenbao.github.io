## 【代数】取整函数方程

**难度：** ⭐⭐⭐（AMC 10/12 高中竞赛难度）

**题目来源：** [2023 AMC 10 part-b Problem 22](https://wiki.randommath.com/amc10/2023/part-b/problem-22)

**知识点：** 取整函数（下取整）· 二次方程 · 分类讨论

**题目：**

（英文原题）Problem: How many distinct values of x satisfy ⌊ x ⌋ 2 − 3 x + 2 = 0, where ⌊ x ⌋ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to x ?  A. an infinite number B.4 C.2 D.3 E.0

（中文翻译）满足方程 $\lfloor x \rfloor^2 - 3x + 2 = 0$ 的不同 $x$ 值共有多少个？其中 $\lfloor x \rfloor$ 表示不超过 $x$ 的最大整数。选项：A. 无穷多个  B. 4  C. 2  D. 3  E. 0

### 常见错误

- 错误1：只考虑了整数情形 $x=k$，漏掉非整数区间 $(k, k+1)$ 内的解，导致少算。
- 错误2：在解 $x = \\dfrac{k^2+2}{3}$ 时，忘记验证区间约束 $k < x < k+1$，把不满足的整数 $k$ 也算进去。
- 错误3：把 $\\lfloor x \\rfloor^2$ 误写成 $\\lfloor x^2 \\rfloor$，概念混淆。

---

### 知识背景

下取整函数 $\\lfloor x \\rfloor$ 表示不超过 $x$ 的最大整数，是数论与离散数学的基础工具。处理含 $\\lfloor x \\rfloor$ 的方程，核心思路是“分段”：令 $\\lfloor x \\rfloor = k$，则 $x \\in [k, k+1)$，原方程化为关于 $k$ 与 $x$ 的关系式，再代回区间约束逐一筛选。这类题训练的是“先分类、后验证”的严谨思维，在 AMC 10/12 中属于中档偏易但极易因粗心丢分的一类。

## 【组合】网格牙签闭合回路计数

**难度：** ⭐⭐⭐⭐（AMC 12 组合计数难题）

**题目来源：** [2024 AMC 12 part-a Problem 22](https://wiki.randommath.com/amc12/2024/part-a/problem-22)

**知识点：** 组合计数 · 网格路径 · 分类讨论 · 对称分析

**题目：**

（英文原题）Problem: The figure below shows a dotted grid 8 cells wide and 3 cells tall consisting of 1 ′ ′ × 1 ′ ′ squares. Carl places 1 -inch toothpicks along some of the sides of the squares to create a closed loop that does not intersect itself. The numbers in the cells indicate the number of sides of that square that are to be covered by toothpicks, and any number of toothpicks are allowed if no number is written. In how many ways can Carl place the toothpicks?  A.130 B.144 C.146 D.162 E.196

（中文翻译）下图是一个宽 8 格、高 3 格的点阵网格，由 $1''\times1''$ 的小正方形组成。Carl 沿着部分小正方形的边放置 1 英寸长的牙签，构成一个不自交的闭合回路。格中的数字表示该正方形应被牙签覆盖的边数（未写数字的格子可任意放置牙签）。问 Carl 共有多少种放置牙签的方式？选项：A. 130  B. 144  C. 146  D. 162  E. 196

---

### 解题过程

There are two possibilities for the loop if it does not cross the middle row of 1 s : an 8 × 1 rectangle around the top row of cells or an 8 × 1 rectangle around the bottom row of cells. Otherwise, wherever the loop crosses the middle row, it must proceed straight in both directions after the middle row. This divides the dotted grid into two connected components, and both ends of the loop must enter the same connected component. It follows that the loop must cross one of the leftmost two columns and one of the rightmost two columns for a total of four configurations. In each case there are two ends of the loop that must connect-one across the top and one across the bottom-along with some number of 1 s in the middle of the grid. Suppose there are n such 1 s . For each 1, the loop can cover either the top edge or the bottom edge. These choices are independent of each other, so the number of ways to connect both ends of the loop is 2 n . The figure below demonstrates one possibility when n = 6. Of the four possibilities for what happens at the ends, one gives n = 6, two give n = 5, and one gives n = 4. The total number of solutions is 26 + 2 ⋅ 25 + 24 + 2 = 64 + 64 + 16 + 2 = ( C ) 146 ​ Note: The rules for laying matchsticks mirror those found in the logic puzzle genre Slitherlink, which originated from Japan in the early 1990s. ### 常见错误

- 错误1：忽略“闭合且不自交”的回路约束，把局部线段随意组合，导致重复或非法计数。
- 错误2：没有利用中间行数字 1 的提示做情形划分，直接暴力枚举而遗漏/重复。
- 错误3：左右对称的两端没有合并统计，或在“跨越中间行”的情形中漏掉左两列与右两列的对称性。

### 知识背景

这道題是典型的“网格上的回路计数”组合题，属于 AMC 12 较难的组合模块。解题关键是把复杂约束拆成互不重叠的情形（不跨中间行 / 跨中间行），再对每种情形利用网格的连通性与对称性分别计数，最后相加。它锻炼的是将几何直观转化为组合分类的能力，也是竞赛中区分高分段选手的重要题型。
