## 本期导读

本日精选 5 篇美联储 FEDS Notes 最新研究笔记，覆盖货币操作框架、银行流动性与监管稳健性等维度。

### 1. 准备金要求取消后的零售扫单行为

- **原文**：Retail Sweep Behavior Since the Elimination of Reserve Requirements
- **来源**：Fed FEDS Notes ｜ **分类**：宏观/货币
- **摘要**：零售扫单（retail sweeps）兴于1990年代，是存款机构规避法定准备金成本的账户安排。尽管2008年付息准备金后成本被压缩，规模仍扩张；本文考察准备金要求被实质性取消后的结构性变化。
- **链接**：[https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/retail-sweep-behavior-since-the-elimination-of-reserve-requirements-20260622.html](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/retail-sweep-behavior-since-the-elimination-of-reserve-requirements-20260622.html)

### 2. 用 N-PORT 数据衡量共同基金流动性

- **原文**：Measuring Mutual Fund Liquidity with N-PORT
- **来源**：Fed FEDS Notes ｜ **分类**：微观/市场
- **摘要**：开放式共同基金是公司债、国债与市政债的主要持有人。压力事件暴露其流动性供给脆弱性——资产难变现却须按需满足大额赎回。本文利用 N-PORT 监管申报数据提出流动性衡量新方法。
- **链接**：[https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/measuring-mutual-fund-liquidity-with-n-port-20260508.html](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/measuring-mutual-fund-liquidity-with-n-port-20260508.html)

### 3. 用 SLOOS 衡量银行放贷标准预期冲击

- **原文**：Measuring Shocks to Banks’ Expectations for Lending Standards Using the Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey
- **来源**：Fed FEDS Notes ｜ **分类**：宏观/银行
- **摘要**：关于银行如何形成对未来信贷供给的预期研究甚少。本文基于美联储高级信贷员意见调查（SLOOS）构建指数，衡量银行对放贷标准与信贷需求变化的预期冲击，揭示经验如何塑造信贷决策。
- **链接**：[https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/measuring-shocks-to-banks-expectations-for-lending-standards-using-the-senior-loan-officer-opinion-survey-20260223.html](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/measuring-shocks-to-banks-expectations-for-lending-standards-using-the-senior-loan-officer-opinion-survey-20260223.html)

### 4. 银行家协会银行在联邦基金市场中的角色

- **原文**：Bankers’ Banks and their Role in the Federal Funds Market
- **来源**：Fed FEDS Notes ｜ **分类**：微观/货币
- **摘要**：全球金融危机与大规模资产购买重塑了美国货币政策框架。2008年前美联储在稀缺准备金体制下引导联邦基金利率；本文考察 banker's banks 在演变后联邦基金市场的中介角色。
- **链接**：[https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/bankers-banks-and-their-role-in-the-federal-funds-market-20260130.html](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/bankers-banks-and-their-role-in-the-federal-funds-market-20260130.html)

### 5. 一级杠杆率与社区银行倒闭：GFC 后的实证

- **原文**：The Empirical Relationship Between the Tier 1 Leverage Ratio and Community Bank Failures after the Global Financial Crisis
- **来源**：Fed FEDS Notes ｜ **分类**：微观/监管
- **摘要**：本文用逻辑回归刻画2008–2012年社区银行倒闭概率，估计一级杠杆率的影响。五年内约5.4%的7184家社区银行倒闭，模型仅凭少数指标即正确分类95%样本，凸显杠杆率的核心作用。
- **链接**：[https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/the-empirical-relationship-between-the-tier-1-leverage-ratio-and-community-bank-failures-after-the-gfc-20251215.html](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/the-empirical-relationship-between-the-tier-1-leverage-ratio-and-community-bank-failures-after-the-gfc-20251215.html)
