# 财经精选 2026-11-07：货币政策、银行体系与企业韧性的多维透视


## 1. 央行资产负债表的社会福利分析

**原文标题：** A welfare analysis of the central bank balance sheet  

**来源：** Bank of England  

**链接：** [https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/working-paper/2026/a-welfare-analysis-of-the-central-bank-balance-sheet](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/working-paper/2026/a-welfare-analysis-of-the-central-bank-balance-sheet)  

**摘要：** 英国央行从福利经济学视角评估其大规模资产负债表运作的收益与成本，探讨非常规货币政策常态化的利弊权衡，为后危机时代的央行框架提供理论支撑。

## 2. 锁定利率还是保持灵活？利率冲击下的按揭再融资行为

**原文标题：** Locking in the rate or staying flexible? Mortgage refinancing around an interest rate shock  

**来源：** Bank of England  

**链接：** [https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/working-paper/2026/locking-in-the-rate-or-staying-flexible-mortgage-refinancing-around-an-interest-rate-shock](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/working-paper/2026/locking-in-the-rate-or-staying-flexible-mortgage-refinancing-around-an-interest-rate-shock)  

**摘要：** 研究家庭在利率冲击来临时如何在固定利率与浮动利率按揭之间抉择，揭示利率风险在家庭部门的传导机制及其对货币政策传导效率的影响。

## 3. 生产率与通胀动态

**原文标题：** Productivity and inflation dynamics  

**来源：** Bank of England  

**链接：** [https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/working-paper/2026/productivity-and-inflation-dynamics](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/working-paper/2026/productivity-and-inflation-dynamics)  

**摘要：** 构建包含资本错配的宏观模型，刻画生产率冲击如何通过资源配置渠道影响通胀路径，为理解低生产率与高通胀并存现象提供新解释。

## 4. 企业如何在实时中应对经济冲击？

**原文标题：** How do firms cope with economic shocks in real time?  

**来源：** Bank of England  

**链接：** [https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/working-paper/2026/how-do-firms-cope-with-economic-shocks-in-real-time](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/working-paper/2026/how-do-firms-cope-with-economic-shocks-in-real-time)  

**摘要：** 基于英国企业高频调查数据，刻画企业在面临突发经济冲击时的即时调整策略（裁员、定价、投资），为微观韧性研究提供实时证据。

## 5. 跨境所有权与投入品获取：来自锂供应链的证据

**原文标题：** Cross-Border Ownership and Input Access: Evidence from the Lithium Supply Chain  

**来源：** arXiv econ.GN  

**链接：** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08430](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08430)  

**摘要：** 以锂供应链为例，实证分析跨国股权控制如何影响关键矿产的投入品获取与贸易流向，揭示地缘经济背景下供应链安全的微观逻辑。
